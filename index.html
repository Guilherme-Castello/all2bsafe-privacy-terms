Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

All2BeSafe respects your privacy and only requests access to device features when they are required to provide application functionality.

Device Permissions

All2BeSafe may request access to your device's camera, microphone, and location.

Camera access may be used when a feature requires the user to capture images within the application.

Microphone access may be used when a feature requires audio input or recording.

Location access may be used when a feature requires the user's current location or location-related functionality.

These permissions are only used to provide the functionality requested by the user and are not used for advertising, tracking, profiling, or marketing purposes.

Personal Data

All2BeSafe does not sell personal information or use personal information for advertising, analytics, profiling, or cross-app tracking.

Third-Party Services

All2BeSafe does not use third-party advertising or tracking services.

Data Retention and Deletion

All2BeSafe does not retain personal information for advertising, analytics, profiling, or tracking purposes.

Users may contact us regarding questions about their data or requests related to privacy.
